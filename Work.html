Community case management of malaria: MEAL across 528 community health units

Provide strategic MEAL leadership for the Global Fund–supported malaria programme, where Amref Health Africa is Principal Recipient and TINADA the Sub-Recipient, designing the monitoring, quality-assurance and analysis that turn monthly community case management (CCMm) data from 528 community health units into decisions supervisors can act on.

Built surveillance indicators: testing rate, test positivity (TPR) and treatment coverage across 528 CHUs
Ran routine data-quality checks with disaggregation by age, sex and sub-county
Computed population-adjusted incidence to compare sub-counties fairly
Turned each month's data into a ranked supervision shortlist, where to visit next
MEAL Malaria CCMm Global Fund Data quality

Implementation Research · Digital Health

Strengthening febrile-illness management with AI-assisted malaria diagnosis

Led the MEL and research coordination for an implementation study delivered under TINADA, the local implementing partner to Medic Afya (the study's donor, funded by the Gates Foundation), evaluating integration of the HealthPulse AI digital mRDT reader into eCHIS, and measuring how AI-assisted diagnosis changes accuracy, health-worker behaviour and cost across community health units.

Generated evidence on mRDT diagnostic accuracy and malaria treatment decision-making
Measured community health promoter (CHP) adoption of digital diagnostic tools
Assessed service-delivery performance and cost-effectiveness of the integration
Fed findings into national policy discussions on digital-health scale-up

Mapping Kenya's aquaculture value chain into a national Blue Book

Designed and executed a national study under the Aquaculture Business Development Programme (ABDP), a Government of Kenya and IFAD initiative, in collaboration with the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Fisheries and Cooperatives, to identify and document key stakeholders and value-chain players across Kenya's aquaculture sector, turning fragmented industry data into a reference adopted for government planning.

Part of my wider MEAL role at ABDP (2020–22).

Documented stakeholders and players across the national aquaculture value chain
Findings informed national aquaculture strategy and improved data accessibility
Culminated in a Blue Book adopted by policymakers for planning & resource allocation
Provided actionable insights supporting sustainable sector growth
