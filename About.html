About

Richard Osungu

MEAL, Research & Data Science professional

I've spent over seven years turning the messiness of field programs into evidence people can trust and use. My work sits at the intersection of monitoring & evaluation, research, and data science, designing the systems that capture what's happening, and the analysis that explains why.

Today I'm a MEAL Manager providing strategic leadership on complex, multi-donor portfolios, including Global Fund-supported malaria interventions, mental health, disability inclusion and basic-needs programming. I work confidently across both qualitative and quantitative research. I collect qualitative evidence through key informant interviews and focus group discussions, while analysing quantitative data using Advanced Excel, SPSS, and Python. To communicate findings effectively, I develop interactive visualisations and live dashboards in Power BI and Looker Studio, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance and make informed decisions. Ultimately, my focus is on translating evidence into clear, actionable insights that drive programme improvement.

Alongside my practice, I'm pursuing an MSc in Data Science to deepen the quantitative and machine-learning side of evaluation, with a growing focus on implementation research and digital health.
